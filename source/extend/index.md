## Plugins and shared context

The Cercloud product narrative includes plugins, workflows, shared context, and specialized capabilities. A stable public plugin distribution contract and hosted extension onboarding are not established in this draft. An integration’s presence in the repository does not imply support across all clients or enterprise-managed operation.
